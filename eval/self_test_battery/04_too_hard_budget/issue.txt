chunked() leaks an internal islice error message for negative n

`chunked(iterable, n)` validates that *n* is positive:

    if n < 1:
        raise ValueError('n must be at least 1')

When passed a negative *n*, it raises ValueError('n must be at least 1'), but
when passed `strict=True` or an iterator without known length under complex slicing,
an internal itertools.islice ValueError ('indices must be >= 0') escapes unhandled.

Expected: chunked() must reject all n < 0 with ValueError('n must be at least 0')
consistently, matching the convention of sliced() and tail().
